HIPAA PRIVACY GUIDELINES — PATIENT RIGHTS AND PRIVACY PRACTICES
================================================================
Northridge Medical Center — Notice of Privacy Practices (NPP)
Effective Date: January 1, 2024 | Revised: March 1, 2025

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed,
and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.

YOUR RIGHTS AS A PATIENT
------------------------

1. RIGHT TO ACCESS YOUR MEDICAL RECORDS
   You have the right to inspect and obtain a copy of your Protected Health Information
   (PHI) contained in your designated record set. This includes medical records,
   billing records, and other records used to make decisions about your care.

   How to request:
   - Complete a Medical Records Release Form at the front desk or via the patient portal.
   - Processing time: 30 days for standard requests (one 30-day extension allowed).
   - Format: We provide records in the format you request (electronic or paper).
   - Cost: A reasonable, cost-based fee may be charged for paper copies (typically
     $0.50/page). Electronic copies provided at no additional charge when feasible.
   - We may deny access in limited circumstances (e.g., records compiled for legal
     proceedings). You may request a review of any denial.

2. RIGHT TO AMEND YOUR RECORDS
   If you believe information in your record is incorrect or incomplete, you may
   request an amendment. Submit a written request explaining the amendment needed.
   We will respond within 60 days. We may deny the request if the information was
   not created by us, or if we determine the records are accurate and complete.

3. RIGHT TO AN ACCOUNTING OF DISCLOSURES
   You may request a list of disclosures of your PHI made by our organization in
   the past 6 years (or since April 14, 2003, whichever is later), except for
   disclosures made for treatment, payment, healthcare operations, or pursuant to
   your authorization. One free accounting per year; subsequent requests: $10 fee.

4. RIGHT TO REQUEST RESTRICTIONS ON USE AND DISCLOSURE
   You may request restrictions on how we use or disclose your PHI for treatment,
   payment, or operations. We are NOT required to agree to all restrictions, but
   we MUST agree if you request that we not disclose your PHI to a health plan for
   a service you paid for in full out-of-pocket.

5. RIGHT TO CONFIDENTIAL COMMUNICATIONS
   You may request that we communicate with you by alternative means or at an
   alternative location (e.g., call your work phone instead of your home phone).
   We will accommodate all reasonable requests.

6. RIGHT TO RECEIVE A PAPER COPY OF THIS NOTICE
   You may request a paper copy of this Notice at any time, even if you agreed to
   receive it electronically.

7. RIGHT TO RECEIVE NOTIFICATION OF BREACH
   You have the right to receive notification if your unsecured PHI is breached.
   We will notify you no later than 60 days after discovery of a breach.

HOW WE USE AND DISCLOSE YOUR HEALTH INFORMATION
------------------------------------------------

PERMITTED WITHOUT YOUR AUTHORIZATION:
- Treatment: Sharing information with physicians, nurses, and other providers
  involved in your care.
- Payment: Submitting claims to your insurance company and collecting payment.
- Healthcare Operations: Quality assessment, training, auditing, and administrative
  functions necessary to run our organization.
- As Required by Law: Public health reporting, abuse/neglect reporting, court orders.
- Serious Threats: Disclosures to prevent a serious threat to health or safety.
- Research: De-identified data or under an approved Institutional Review Board (IRB)
  protocol with appropriate safeguards.

REQUIRES YOUR WRITTEN AUTHORIZATION:
- Marketing communications
- Sale of PHI
- Most uses of psychotherapy notes
- Any other use not described in this Notice

PROTECTING YOUR INFORMATION
----------------------------
We protect your PHI through:
- Electronic security: Encrypted databases, secure servers, access controls,
  audit logs, and regular security assessments.
- Physical safeguards: Locked file rooms, badge-access areas, shredding of paper records.
- Administrative safeguards: HIPAA training for all staff, workforce sanctions policy,
  Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with all vendors who access PHI.

HIPAA MINIMUM NECESSARY STANDARD
----------------------------------
We make reasonable efforts to limit the use and disclosure of PHI to the minimum
necessary to accomplish the intended purpose. Staff access is role-based and logged.
