PATIENT DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS
================================
Northridge Medical Center — Patient Care Services

Thank you for choosing Northridge Medical Center for your care. These instructions
are designed to help you recover safely at home. Please read them carefully and
share them with your caregiver or family members.

WOUND CARE
----------
- Keep the incision site clean and dry for the first 48 hours after discharge.
- After 48 hours, you may gently clean the area with mild soap and water; pat dry.
- Change dressings as instructed by your nurse. Use only the supplies provided.
- Do NOT remove steri-strips or wound closure strips; allow them to fall off naturally.
- Watch for signs of infection: increased redness, swelling, warmth, discharge with
  odor, or wound opening. Report any of these immediately.

ACTIVITY RESTRICTIONS
---------------------
- Do not drive for at least 24 hours after receiving anesthesia or narcotic pain
  medication. Arrange for someone to drive you home.
- Avoid lifting objects heavier than 10 pounds for the first two weeks, unless your
  surgeon instructs otherwise.
- Short, gentle walks are encouraged starting the day after discharge to prevent
  blood clots.
- Do not engage in strenuous exercise, swimming, or soaking in a bathtub until cleared
  by your physician (typically 4–6 weeks depending on procedure type).
- Resume normal activities gradually as your energy and comfort allow.

MEDICATIONS
-----------
- Take all prescribed medications as directed. Do not skip doses.
- For pain management, start with over-the-counter options (acetaminophen or ibuprofen)
  if adequate; use prescribed opioids only if necessary and for the shortest duration.
- Do not consume alcohol while taking prescription pain medications.
- If you were taking blood thinners prior to surgery, confirm with your doctor when
  to restart them.
- Bring a complete list of your medications to all follow-up appointments.

DIET AND HYDRATION
------------------
- Begin with clear liquids and advance to regular food as tolerated unless a special
  diet was prescribed.
- Stay well hydrated — aim for 6–8 glasses of water per day.
- Avoid high-sodium and high-fat foods during initial recovery to minimize swelling.

FOLLOW-UP APPOINTMENTS
-----------------------
- You MUST attend your scheduled follow-up appointment within 7–14 days.
- If you do not receive a call within 48 hours to schedule a follow-up, call the
  clinic directly at (555) 200-3000.
- Bring this discharge summary and your medication list to every appointment.

WHEN TO CALL YOUR DOCTOR (555) 200-3001
-----------------------------------------
Contact your physician if you experience any of the following:
- Temperature above 101°F (38.3°C)
- Uncontrolled pain not relieved by prescribed medication
- Nausea or vomiting preventing you from keeping medications down
- Constipation lasting more than 3 days (common after opioid use)
- Redness, swelling, or drainage from the wound site

WHEN TO CALL 911 OR GO TO THE EMERGENCY ROOM
---------------------------------------------
Seek IMMEDIATE emergency care if you experience:
- Chest pain or pressure
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
- Signs of blood clot in the leg: calf pain, swelling, warmth, or redness
- Sudden confusion, weakness, or difficulty speaking
- Heavy or uncontrolled bleeding
- Loss of consciousness
