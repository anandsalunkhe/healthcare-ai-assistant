APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING POLICY
==============================
Northridge Medical Center — Patient Access Services

SCHEDULING AN APPOINTMENT
--------------------------
New and established patients may schedule appointments through any of the following:

  1. Online Patient Portal: portal.northridgemedical.example
     - Available 24/7; fastest method for non-urgent scheduling.
     - Create an account with your date of birth and medical record number.

  2. Phone: Call (555) 800-1234, Monday–Friday 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
     - After-hours urgent care line: (555) 800-9911 (24/7).

  3. In-Person: Front desk at any of our three clinic locations.

  4. Mobile App: Download "NorthridgeCare" from the App Store or Google Play.

APPOINTMENT TYPES
-----------------
- ROUTINE / PREVENTIVE: Annual physicals, wellness exams, immunizations.
  Schedule at least 2 weeks in advance.

- FOLLOW-UP: Post-hospitalization or post-procedure check-ins.
  Schedule within 7–14 days of discharge; call (555) 800-1234 if not contacted.

- URGENT (SAME-DAY / NEXT-DAY): Acute illness, minor injury, sudden worsening of
  a chronic condition. Call by 8:30 AM for same-day availability; slots are limited.

- TELEHEALTH: Video consultations available for eligible conditions.
  See Telehealth Policy document for details.

- SPECIALIST REFERRALS: Your primary care provider will coordinate specialist
  appointments. Expect a referral authorization within 2–5 business days.

INSURANCE VERIFICATION
-----------------------
- Please provide your current insurance card and a government-issued photo ID at
  every appointment.
- Insurance eligibility is verified at least 24 hours before your appointment.
- If your insurance has changed, notify us at least 48 hours in advance to avoid
  billing issues.
- Copays and coinsurance are due at time of service.

CANCELLATION AND NO-SHOW POLICY
---------------------------------
Cancellations:
- Please cancel at least 24 hours before your scheduled appointment.
- To cancel: use the patient portal, call (555) 800-1234, or use the mobile app.
- Late cancellations (less than 24 hours) may incur a $25 administrative fee.

No-Shows:
- A no-show fee of $40 may be charged for missed appointments without prior notice.
- Three or more no-shows within a 12-month period may result in discharge from the
  practice (patients will be given 30 days' notice and assistance finding new care).

Emergency Exceptions:
- Fees are waived for documented medical emergencies or natural disasters.
- Contact our billing department within 30 days to request a waiver.

ARRIVAL INSTRUCTIONS
--------------------
- Arrive 15 minutes early for new patient appointments to complete paperwork.
- Arrive 10 minutes early for established patient appointments.
- Late arrival (more than 15 minutes) may result in rescheduling to protect other
  patients' appointment times.
- Parking is available in the Main Garage (validated for 3 hours with appointment).

MINORS AND GUARDIANSHIP
-----------------------
- Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at all visits,
  unless an Unaccompanied Minor Consent Form is on file.
- Legal guardians must present documentation at the first visit.

PATIENT RIGHTS DURING SCHEDULING
----------------------------------
- You have the right to choose your preferred provider subject to availability.
- You have the right to request a same-gender provider when clinically appropriate.
- Language interpretation services are available at no charge — request when scheduling.
- Accessible appointment times and accommodations for disabilities are available
  upon request.
