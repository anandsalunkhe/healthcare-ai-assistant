INSURANCE ELIGIBILITY FAQ
==========================
Northridge Medical Center — Billing and Insurance Services

GENERAL COVERAGE QUESTIONS
---------------------------

Q: What insurance plans does Northridge Medical Center accept?
A: We accept most major commercial insurance plans, Medicare, Medicaid, and CHIP.
   A current list of accepted insurers is available at billing@northridgemedical.example
   or by calling (555) 700-2000. Always verify your specific plan directly with us
   before your appointment, as plan acceptance can change.

Q: How do I find out if my specific plan is in-network?
A: Call the Member Services number on the back of your insurance card and ask whether
   Northridge Medical Center (NPI: 1234567890) is in-network for your plan. You can
   also ask our billing team at (555) 700-2000 to perform an eligibility check.

Q: What is a copay, and when is it due?
A: A copay is a fixed dollar amount you pay for a covered service at the time of
   the visit (e.g., $20 for a primary care visit, $50 for a specialist). Copays are
   due at check-in and are listed on your insurance card.

Q: What is a deductible?
A: A deductible is the amount you pay out-of-pocket for covered services before your
   insurance begins to pay. For example, if your deductible is $1,500, you pay the
   first $1,500 of covered services each plan year; after that, your insurer shares
   the cost. Preventive services (annual physicals, immunizations) are often covered
   before the deductible is met — check your plan documents.

Q: What is coinsurance?
A: Coinsurance is your share of the cost after meeting your deductible, expressed as
   a percentage. For example, with 20% coinsurance, you pay 20% of the allowed amount
   and your insurer pays 80%.

Q: What is an out-of-pocket maximum?
A: This is the most you will pay in a plan year for covered services. Once you reach
   this amount, your insurance pays 100% for covered services for the rest of the year.
   This does not include premiums or non-covered services.

PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
-------------------

Q: What services require prior authorization?
A: Many specialist visits, diagnostic imaging (MRI, CT scans), elective procedures,
   durable medical equipment, and certain prescription medications require prior
   authorization from your insurer before the service is provided.

Q: Who is responsible for obtaining prior authorization?
A: Our clinic obtains prior authorization on your behalf for services we order.
   However, you are ultimately responsible for ensuring authorization is obtained.
   Always verify authorization before a scheduled procedure.

Q: What happens if I receive a service without prior authorization?
A: Your insurer may deny the claim, leaving you responsible for the full cost.
   If authorization is denied, you have the right to appeal. Contact our billing
   team within 10 business days of a denial notice.

Q: How long does prior authorization take?
A: Routine requests: 3–5 business days. Urgent/urgent requests: 24–72 hours.
   Emergency services are covered without prior authorization.

BILLING AND CLAIMS
------------------

Q: When will I receive a bill?
A: After your insurer processes the claim (typically 30–45 days after your visit),
   you will receive an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) from your insurer and a
   statement from us showing your balance due.

Q: What if I cannot afford my bill?
A: We offer payment plans (0% interest, up to 12 months) and financial assistance
   programs for qualifying patients. Call our Financial Counseling team at
   (555) 700-3000. Sliding-scale fee assistance is available for uninsured patients.

Q: How do I dispute a charge?
A: Review your EOB from your insurer and compare it to our bill. If you believe
   there is an error, call (555) 700-2000 within 90 days of the billing date.
   Provide your account number, the date of service, and a description of the dispute.

UNINSURED PATIENTS
------------------

Q: What options are available if I have no insurance?
A: We provide care to all patients regardless of ability to pay.
   - Self-pay discount: A 30% discount is automatically applied to uninsured patients.
   - Charity care: Patients at or below 200% of the federal poverty level may qualify
     for free or reduced-cost care. Contact Financial Counseling: (555) 700-3000.
   - Payment plans: Flexible monthly payment options available.
   - Marketplace enrollment assistance: We can connect you with a certified enrollment
     counselor at no charge.
