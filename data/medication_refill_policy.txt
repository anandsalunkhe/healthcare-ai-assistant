MEDICATION REFILL POLICY
========================
Northridge Medical Center — Pharmacy and Prescribing Services

REQUESTING A REFILL
--------------------
We offer several convenient ways to request prescription refills:

  1. Patient Portal (PREFERRED): portal.northridgemedical.example
     - Submit a refill request 7–10 days before you run out.
     - You will receive a portal message when the prescription is sent to your pharmacy.

  2. Pharmacy Auto-Refill: Contact your pharmacy directly. Many pharmacies offer
     automatic refill programs. The pharmacy will contact us for authorization.

  3. Phone: Call (555) 500-2200 (Refill Request Line), Monday–Friday 8 AM–5 PM.
     - Leave your name, date of birth, medication name, pharmacy name and phone.
     - Allow 48–72 business hours for processing (not including weekends/holidays).

  4. Mobile App: Use the NorthridgeCare app to submit refill requests 24/7.

PROCESSING TIMEFRAMES
----------------------
- Routine refills: 48–72 business hours
- Controlled substances: Require an in-person or telehealth visit; see below.
- Specialty medications: May require prior authorization (5–10 business days).
- Do NOT wait until you run out of medication — always request 7–10 days early.

IMPORTANT: We cannot process same-day urgent refill requests through the refill line.
If you have run out of a critical medication (e.g., insulin, blood pressure medication),
call the main clinic line at (555) 500-0000 immediately for same-day assistance.

CONTROLLED SUBSTANCE POLICY
----------------------------
In compliance with federal DEA regulations and California state law:

- Controlled substances (Schedule II–V) require a valid current prescription.
- Schedule II medications (e.g., Adderall, oxycodone) CANNOT be called in or faxed;
  they require a written or electronic prescription (EPCS) from your provider.
- An in-person or telehealth visit is REQUIRED for refills of Schedule II medications.
- Schedule III–V medications may be called in for up to a 6-month supply, provided
  a current assessment has been completed within the past 12 months.
- We do NOT prescribe opioids for new patients without a comprehensive evaluation.
- Refills may be denied if a patient misses follow-up visits or does not comply with
  monitoring requirements (e.g., urine drug screens for opioid agreements).

EARLY REFILL RESTRICTIONS
--------------------------
- Insurance plans typically apply a "too early" fill restriction (e.g., no refill until
  75% of the days' supply has elapsed).
- Patients requesting early refills without documented medical reason (e.g., travel,
  dose change) will be directed to contact their insurer or pay out-of-pocket.
- Lost or stolen medications: Report to law enforcement first. A police report number
  is required for controlled substances before a replacement will be considered.

MEDICATION ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS
--------------------------------
If you cannot afford your medications, we can help:

- Generic substitution: Ask your pharmacist or provider about lower-cost generics.
- Patient Assistance Programs (PAPs): Many manufacturers provide free or discounted
  brand-name medications to qualifying patients. Contact our Social Work team:
  (555) 500-5500.
- 340B Drug Pricing Program: Eligible patients may qualify for significant prescription
  discounts through our 340B program. Ask our pharmacy team at (555) 500-4400.
- GoodRx and similar discount cards: Can reduce out-of-pocket costs at most pharmacies.

MEDICATION CHANGES AND THERAPEUTIC SUBSTITUTIONS
--------------------------------------------------
- If your insurance does not cover a prescribed medication, your pharmacist will
  contact us for a therapeutic substitution or prior authorization.
- Do NOT switch medications or stop taking a medication without consulting your provider.
- Report any adverse reactions or side effects to your provider immediately.
