TELEHEALTH GUIDELINES
======================
Northridge Medical Center — Virtual Care Services

WHAT IS TELEHEALTH?
--------------------
Telehealth (also called telemedicine or virtual care) allows you to have a medical
appointment with your provider using video conferencing or telephone — from your
home, office, or any private location. Northridge Medical Center uses a secure,
HIPAA-compliant video platform integrated with your patient portal.

ELIGIBLE CONDITIONS FOR TELEHEALTH
------------------------------------
Telehealth is appropriate for many common conditions. Examples include:

  - Cold, flu, or mild respiratory symptoms
  - Urinary tract infection (UTI) — evaluation and prescription
  - Allergies and rashes (with photo submission via portal)
  - Mental health consultations: anxiety, depression, medication follow-up
  - Medication refills and management for stable chronic conditions (e.g., hypertension,
    diabetes, hypothyroidism — when labs are current)
  - Post-procedure follow-up when physical exam is not required
  - Care coordination, referrals, and care plan reviews
  - Nutrition counseling and health coaching

CONDITIONS REQUIRING IN-PERSON VISITS
---------------------------------------
Telehealth is NOT appropriate for:
  - Chest pain, shortness of breath, or suspected heart attack → Call 911
  - Suspected fractures, deep lacerations, or injuries requiring imaging
  - Physical examinations requiring auscultation or palpation
  - New patients requiring a comprehensive baseline physical
  - Controlled substance prescriptions (Schedule II) — see Medication Refill Policy
  - Pediatric well-child visits requiring growth measurements and vaccinations
  - Procedures, injections, or specimen collection

HOW TO CONNECT — STEP BY STEP
-------------------------------
1. SCHEDULE: Book a telehealth appointment via the patient portal or call (555) 800-1234.
   Select "Virtual Visit" as the appointment type.

2. CONFIRM: You will receive an email and/or text with a secure video link 24 hours
   and 1 hour before your appointment.

3. PREPARE — 15 minutes before your visit:
   - Find a private, quiet, well-lit space.
   - Have your medication bottles on hand.
   - Make a list of symptoms, questions, and any recent test results.
   - Test your camera and microphone using the "Test My Device" link in the email.

4. JOIN: Click the secure link in your email or log into the patient portal and click
   "Join Video Visit." No app download required; works in any modern web browser.
   Supported browsers: Chrome 80+, Firefox 75+, Safari 13+, Edge 80+.

5. WAIT ROOM: You will enter a virtual waiting room. Your provider will join within
   the appointment window.

TECHNOLOGY REQUIREMENTS
------------------------
- Device: Computer, laptop, tablet, or smartphone
- Camera and microphone (built-in or external)
- Internet: Broadband (minimum 1.5 Mbps upload/download) or strong 4G/LTE signal
- Browser: Up-to-date Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge
- If using a smartphone: Ensure camera permissions are enabled for the browser.

TROUBLESHOOTING TIPS
---------------------
- Camera not working? Check browser permissions: Settings → Privacy → Camera.
- Audio issues? Try using headphones to reduce echo.
- Poor connection? Move closer to your router or switch to a wired connection.
- Can't join the visit? Call our telehealth support line: (555) 800-8800.
  Providers can convert the visit to a phone call if technical issues persist.

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
---------------------
- All video visits are conducted through an end-to-end encrypted platform (AES-256).
- Visits are NOT recorded unless you provide explicit written consent.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi (coffee shops, airports) for telehealth visits.
- Ensure no other individuals (except caregivers or interpreters you invite) are
  present during your visit without your provider's knowledge.
- Providers will verify your identity at the start of every visit.

BILLING AND INSURANCE
----------------------
- Telehealth visits are billed the same as in-person visits for most services.
- Many insurance plans, including Medicare and Medicaid, cover telehealth visits.
- Copays for telehealth may differ from in-person visits — verify with your insurer.
- You will receive a summary visit note and any new prescriptions via the portal
  within 24 hours of your visit.
